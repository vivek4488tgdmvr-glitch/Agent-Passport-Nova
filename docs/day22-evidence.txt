DAY 22 — CRYPTOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE

Passport Travel evidence is now an independently verifiable artifact.

Evidence is canonicalized and SHA-256 hashed, then signed with Ed25519.
The signed envelope records the Passport fingerprint, issuer, public key,
and signature. Verification checks the evidence hash, Ed25519 signature,
and the expected Passport fingerprint.

This creates an explicit trust link:
Passport -> fingerprint -> Travel evidence -> signature -> verification

The evidence signature does not establish issuer trust by itself. Trust in
an issuer/key remains an external policy decision, just as with Passport
signatures.

CLI:
  agent-passport sign-evidence evidence.json passport.yaml PRIVATE_KEY ISSUER signed.json
  agent-passport verify-evidence signed.json passport.yaml
